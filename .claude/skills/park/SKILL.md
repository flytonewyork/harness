# Park an idea

1. Add one line under "Parked until the 1 Feb 2027 review" in `NOW.md`: the idea in Thomas's words and today's date.
2. Open a small PR titled `Park: <idea>`. Don't argue its merits, and don't start work on it.
3. Reply with the PR link.

This is the parking-lot rule: anything that tempts a change of direction waits for the 1 Feb review.
