## Likely questions, short answers

  with rough sizes, how it would connect to the EMR, the demo rebuilt on EC's workflow, and a scoped next step.
- **Do you need patient data?** No. No records, exports, chart screenshots, accounts or access. Notes keep workflow
  and counts only, never names, IDs or clinical details.
- **Will you contact patients or install anything?** No, neither.
- **How much staff time?** Half a day at one clinic, 30 minutes with IT, one contact.
- **What does it cost?** A fixed fee, in the written scope by Thursday, invoiced on delivery.
- **How would it touch our EMR?** Reads what it needs, writes back only after a doctor signs off, runs inside EC's
  environment. The discovery maps exactly this before anything is built.
- **Is it safe? Does AI make clinical decisions?** It can only open items, raise urgency or alarm. It can never close,
  dismiss or reassure; only doctors close items. Every item shows the exact quotes it is based on.
- **What comes after?** Most likely the safety analysis: a look-back EC's IT runs inside EC, so nothing leaves EC. Or
  the new GP practice, if it goes ahead.
- **Can our doctors try it?** Yes, the rebuilt demo with made-up patients is part of the findings.
- **Is the care line live?** Not for EC. It's a doctor's WhatsApp line in testing; don't offer it as running.
