## What it's for
Find out, at one EC clinic, which follow-up and front-desk work costs EC's doctors and staff the most, and whether
tmrw's care line and clinic backend would take it off them safely. It answers one question before anything is built
or any record is read: what should go first?

## What EC gets, within three weeks of the visit
- **A map of the clinic's follow-up work:** how results, letters, referrals, recalls and patient messages arrive, who
  handles each, and where they wait.
- **The top three burdens** on the doctors and the front desk, each with a rough size (time per week, or items per
  week) taken from what staff say and show us, not from records.
- **How it would connect to the EMR:** what it would read, what it would write back after sign-off, what it would
  never do, and that it runs inside EC's environment.
- **The demo rebuilt around EC's own workflow**, with made-up patients, so EC's doctors can try it.
- **A proposed next step** with its own scope. Most likely the safety analysis (a look-back that EC's IT runs inside
  EC's environment, so nothing leaves EC), or the new GP practice if it goes ahead.
