Care line privacy notice
護理熱線私隱聲明

Who we are

The care line is an automated WhatsApp service run by tmrw (TMRW CONSULTING LIMITED) for your doctor. Your doctor decides what the line is used for, and is responsible for your medical care and records. tmrw processes your information on your doctor's behalf, under the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance.

What we collect

  • Your WhatsApp number, the name and date of birth you give us, and your language.
  • The messages, photos and documents you send, and the messages we send you.
  • Your plan and replies, written from your doctor's own words and approved by your doctor.
  • If you pay for a follow-up: what you paid for and when. Card details go to Stripe, never to us.

How we use it

  • To send you your doctor's plan, check-ins, reminders and replies, and to pass your messages to your doctor.
  • To draft text for your doctor to check. We use Google's Vertex AI service (Gemini models) for drafting. A draft never reaches you unless your doctor approves it, and the model never writes to you on its own.
  • We don't sell your information, and we don't use it for advertising.

Where it is kept

On Google Cloud, in the Hong Kong region. Voice notes and photos are deleted 30 days after they are used. Your messages are kept as part of your care record for as long as your doctor needs them for your medical records, then deleted. Your doctor can export your record into their clinical notes.

Who sees it

Your doctor, and the small team at tmrw who run the line, only when needed to keep it working. Meta (WhatsApp) carries the messages. Stripe handles payments. Google hosts the service and the drafting model.

Your choices

  • Write STOP at any time to stop messages. Write JOIN to start again.
  • You can ask to see or correct your information, or ask us to delete what isn't part of your medical record: email me@thomashu.com or ask your doctor.
  • The care line isn't for emergencies. If it can't wait, call 999 or go to A&E.
