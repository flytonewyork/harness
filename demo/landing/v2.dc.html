The problem

Care stops when the patient leaves the room.

Up to 62% of results go unchased Across studies of outpatient care, 7% to 62% of test results were never followed up. They sit in a portal or a fax tray, and the patient never hears.
80% see another doctor within a year Even patients with a regular doctor in Hong Kong try someone else. Symptoms that don't settle are the reason they most often give.
24/7 your WhatsApp never closes Bookings, result questions and repeat requests arrive day and night. Someone has to answer every one, and it is usually you or your nurse.

How it works

Three steps. No new software.

1 Patients join after the visit Ask one question at the end of the consult. They scan a QR code at the desk and they're on your clinic's care line. Nothing to download.
2 The care team follows up Result invitations, check-ins after a visit, chronic reviews and vaccine reminders go out in Cantonese or English, using replies you've approved.
3 You approve. They come back. Anything clinical waits for your one-tap approval. Booked visits land in your diary, and the care team chases until each one is done.

Questions doctors ask

Will my patients actually use it?

It runs on WhatsApp, which most people in Hong Kong already use every day. Patients join by scanning a QR code at your desk. There's nothing to download.

Is it allowed?

The Medical Council's telemedicine guidelines ask that you identify the patient, make the clinical decisions and keep records. tmrw keeps all three with you: you approve anything clinical, and every message is on record.

What will it say to my patients?

Only replies you've approved, in Cantonese or English. Anything else waits for you. Red-flag words get a fixed instruction to call 999, and you're alerted.

How much of my time does it take?

Routine messages never reach you. The ones that need you take one tap: approve, edit or see the patient in person.

Do I need to change my clinic system?

No. During the pilot it runs alongside the system you already use, on your clinic's own WhatsApp number.

What about patient data?

Patients agree before anything is sent. During the pilot, conversations stay in your clinic's own WhatsApp.
