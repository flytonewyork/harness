Privacy policy

Last updated 9 November 2026

tmrw runs a WhatsApp care line for family doctors' clinics in Hong Kong. This policy explains what personal data we handle, why, and the choices you have. We follow the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (Cap. 486).

Who is responsible

Your clinic is responsible for your care and for your medical records; it is the data user. tmrw runs the care line for the clinic and handles your information only on the clinic's instructions and for the purposes below. Questions about this site, or about how tmrw handles data, go to us; questions about your medical records can also go to your clinic.

What we collect

  • Patients who join a clinic's care line: your name, WhatsApp number and date of birth; the messages you exchange with the care line; and the parts of your clinic record the care line needs, such as appointments, test results, prescriptions and your doctor's follow-up plan.
  • Family members or carers a patient has said may help: their name, WhatsApp number and their relationship to the patient.
  • Clinic staff and doctors: name, role, contact details and the approvals they give in tmrw.
  • Visitors to this website: we use no cookies, analytics or tracking. Our host (Vercel) and our font provider (Google Fonts) receive your IP address and browser details when the page loads, as any web server does. If you email us, we keep your email.

Why we use it

  • To run the care line for your clinic: front-desk requests, booking, reminders, follow-up after a visit, results and care after a hospital stay.
  • To check it is you (name and date of birth) before sending a result or a document.
  • To keep a record of every message, check and approval, so your clinic can see who did what and when.
  • To keep the service safe and working, and to measure whether follow-up is happening.

Anything clinical is approved by your doctor before it is sent. Sensitive results (such as sexual health, pregnancy, HIV or cancer) are never named in a message; your doctor talks them through with you.

Your consent and invitations

You agree when you join a clinic's care line. Invitations, such as flu vaccine reminders, are direct marketing under Part 6A of the Ordinance: you get them only if you say yes to them separately, and you can withdraw that yes at any time. Send STOP and the messages stop. Your clinic can still phone you about anything urgent.

Who we share it with

  • Your clinic and the doctors and staff caring for you.
  • Family members or carers, only if you have said so.
  • Service providers that process data for us under contract, such as WhatsApp (Meta) to deliver messages, and cloud hosting and software providers that store and process data for the care line. They may only use it to provide their service to us.
  • Anyone the law requires us to disclose it to.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for anyone else's marketing. Some providers may store data outside Hong Kong; we use them only where they protect it to a standard comparable to the Ordinance.

How long we keep it

Care line messages are kept with your clinic's records, like your doctor's notes, for as long as the clinic must keep medical records. When a clinic stops using tmrw, we return or delete its data as the clinic instructs. Website enquiries are deleted when no longer needed.

How we protect it

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, access is limited to the people who need it, and every access and action is logged.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your personal data and ask us to correct it, under sections 18 and 22 of the Ordinance. You can also ask your clinic. We may charge a fee that is not excessive for a copy, as the Ordinance allows, and we reply within 40 days.

Contact

Privacy questions and data requests: me@thomashu.com. You may also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner for Personal Data, Hong Kong (www.pcpd.org.hk).

Changes

We will post any change on this page with a new date. If a change affects how we use patients' data, clinics will tell their patients before it applies.

tmrw is not an emergency service. In an emergency, call 999.
