What you can do

Everything you'd phone the clinic for.

Open the menu on your clinic's WhatsApp and choose what you need.

Book or change a visit See free times and book in a few taps, with a reminder the day before.
Your plan in writing What your doctor said, on your phone before you reach the lift. Read it again any time.
Is my result back? See whether it has arrived and whether your doctor has seen it. Your doctor goes through it with you.
Repeat prescription Ask for more of your regular medicine. Your doctor checks and signs it, or asks to see you first.
Blood tests and scans How to prepare and where to go, for the test your doctor ordered.
Home from hospital Send a photo of your discharge letter. Your doctor reads it, and you get a follow-up visit.
Receipts and letters Copies of receipts, sick leave and referral letters from your visit.
Fees and vouchers Your clinic's fees, and how to use health care vouchers there.
Talk to the clinic Ask for a person. Your clinic's team replies in opening hours.

Opening hours

It keeps your clinic's hours.

The care line is not an emergency service. Here's what happens when you write.

Any time

Front-desk requests

Booking, opening hours, receipts, and whether your result is back are answered straight away.

Clinic hours

Questions about your health

Questions about symptoms, medicines or results go to your doctor or nurse. You'll be told who will reply and when.

Emergency

Call 999 or go to A&E

Don't wait for a WhatsApp reply. If you write about chest pain, trouble breathing or another danger sign, the reply tells you to call 999 now, and your clinic is told.

Safety

Your doctor stays in charge.

It's an assistant, and it says so The care team is software working for your clinic. Every message says whether it's from the care team, the clinic's staff or your doctor.
Your doctor approves your care Your plan, repeat prescriptions and invitations to discuss a result are approved by your doctor before they reach you.
It never diagnoses or prescribes It won't tell you what a symptom or a result means, or change a medicine. That stays with your doctor.
A person is always there Choose "Talk to the clinic" and the team replies in opening hours. You can still phone or walk in.

Your information

Your messages, your choice.

You choose what you get You agree when you join. Invitations, like flu vaccine reminders, come only if you say yes to them separately.
It checks it's you Before sending a result or a document, it checks your name and date of birth.
Private results stay private Sensitive results are never named in a message. Your doctor talks them through with you.
Family can help A son, daughter or carer can book or get reminders for you, but only if you say so.
Stop any time Send STOP and the messages stop. Your clinic can still phone you about anything urgent.
Kept with your records Your messages are kept with your clinic's records, like your doctor's notes.

How to join

Join at your next visit.

  1. 1Say yes at the end of your visitYour doctor or the front desk will offer it. It's your choice.
  2. 2Scan the QR code at the deskWhatsApp opens with your clinic's care line. Nothing to download.
  3. 3Confirm it's youSend your name and date of birth, and choose what you'd like to receive.

Questions patients ask

Am I talking to a person?

Mostly to the care team, an automated assistant for your clinic. Every message says who sent it, and you can ask for a person at any time.

Does it cost anything?

The care line is free to use. Visits and medicines are charged by your clinic as usual.

What if it's an emergency?

Call 999 or go to A&E. Don't wait for a reply on WhatsApp.

Which languages can I use?

Cantonese or English. For anything else, ask for the clinic's team.

Can I still phone or walk in?

Of course. The care line is an extra way to reach your clinic, not the only one.

How do I stop?

Send STOP. You can join again at your next visit.

Want this at your clinic?

Ask your doctor about tmrw at your next visit. Doctors can start with a free pilot.
