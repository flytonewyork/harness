The problem

Care stops when the patient leaves the room.

Up to 62% of results go unchased Across studies of outpatient care, 7% to 62% of test results were never followed up. The patient never hears.
40–80% of what patients hear is forgotten Much of what doctors tell patients is forgotten straight away. Advice that's written down is remembered better.
41% leave hospital with results pending In a study of 2,644 discharges, results came back after 41% of patients had gone home. Doctors often missed the ones that needed action.
80% see another doctor within a year Even patients with a regular doctor in Hong Kong try someone else, most often because their symptoms didn't settle.

How it works

Three steps. No new software.

1 Patients join after the visit Ask one question at the end of the consult. They scan a QR code at the desk and they're on your clinic's care line. Nothing to download.
2 The care team follows up Plans, test preparation, repeat requests, check-ins and hospital follow-up, in Cantonese or English, using replies you've approved.
3 You approve. They come back. Anything clinical waits for your one-tap approval. Booked visits land in your diary, and the care team chases until each one is done.

Talk to us

Questions doctors ask

Will patients expect replies at 3am?

No. The care line keeps your hours and tells patients so when they join. At night it finishes front-desk requests, holds clinical questions for the morning with a 999 safety net, and sends a fixed reply to anything urgent.

Who's responsible for what it says?

You are, as for anything your clinic sends. That's why it never gives medical advice of its own: it uses replies you've approved and puts everything clinical in front of you.

What about critical results at night?

tmrw doesn't change how your lab reaches you about a critical result. It also puts the report at the top of your queue, and the patient hears nothing until you've seen it.

Will my patients actually use it?

It runs on WhatsApp, which most people in Hong Kong already use every day. Patients join by scanning a QR code at your desk, and older patients can add a son or daughter to help.

Do patients know it's not a person?

Yes. They're told when they join, every message says who sent it, and "Talk to the clinic" reaches your staff.

Is it allowed?

The Medical Council's telemedicine guidelines ask that you identify the patient, make the clinical decisions and keep records. tmrw keeps all three with you, and messages to patients stay factual, with no offers or inducements.

How much of my time does it take?

Front-desk requests never reach you. Clinical ones take one tap: approve, edit or see the patient in person.

Do I need to change my clinic system?

No. It runs alongside the system you already use, on your clinic's WhatsApp Business number, and your staff can keep answering in the app.

What about patient data?

Patients agree before anything is sent, and choose separately whether to get invitations such as flu vaccines. Sensitive results are never named in a message.
