How accurate this is

On a simulated clinic every answer is known, so the look-back can be scored. Of the 375 missed follow-ups the records can show, it found 97%. Of its 375 definite flags, 366 were right (98%). Counting the 107 the records can't show at all, it found 76%. Of the 76 it couldn't judge, 76 had in fact been missed.

When a follow-up counts as owed

  • An abnormal result is acted on within 1, 2, 7 or 14 days, from critical to routine.
  • A test is resulted within 21 days of the order.
  • A referral gets a reply within 14 days if urgent, 90 otherwise.
  • A positive FIT leads to a colonoscopy within 180 days.
  • An abnormal screening mammogram leads to more imaging within 60 days.
  • Monitoring bloods after a new medicine get 14 days' grace.
  • A planned review is overdue 14 days after its date.
